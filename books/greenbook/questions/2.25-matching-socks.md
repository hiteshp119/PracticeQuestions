## Problem

A drawer has 2 red, 20 yellow and 31 blue socks. Grabbing blindly, what's the minimum number you must take to be sure of a matching pair?

## Solution

The worst case is one of each colour: 3 socks, no pair. The 4th sock must match one of them:

$$\text{socks} = \#\text{colours} + 1 = 3 + 1 = 4.$$

## Intuition

**Naive approach.** Use the counts: compute probabilities, or worry about running out of red.

**Why it fails.** "Guarantee" means worst case, and the worst case only depends on the number of colours.

**The trick.** Pigeonhole: $k$ boxes and $k+1$ items ⇒ some box holds 2. Variants: "a pair of a *specific* colour" (red: $20+31+2 = 53$ socks) or "two pairs".
