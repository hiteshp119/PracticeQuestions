## Problem

A wall clock numbered 1 to 12 falls and breaks into three pieces. The numbers on each piece add up to the same total. Which numbers are on each piece? (No strange jigsaw shapes: think of two straight-ish cracks.)

## Solution

$$\sum_{n=1}^{12} n = \frac{12\cdot 13}{2} = 78 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{each piece} = 26.$$

1. $5+6+7+8 = 26$.
2. Using the wrap-around: $11+12+1+2 = 26$.
3. What's left: $3+4+9+10 = 26$.

With two roughly parallel cracks (one between 2|3 and 10|11, one between 4|5 and 8|9) the middle piece is a band across the face: 3, 4 on the right and 9, 10 on the left.

## Intuition

**Naive approach.** Look for runs of consecutive numbers that sum to 26; only $5$–$8$ works on a straight line.

**Why it fails.** It forgets the dial is a circle.

**The trick.** Drop the hidden assumption that each piece is a run of consecutive numbers. Pair numbers that sum to 13 ($12+1$, $11+2$, $10+3$, $9+4$): two such pairs make 26, and the pieces become bands across the face rather than wedges.
