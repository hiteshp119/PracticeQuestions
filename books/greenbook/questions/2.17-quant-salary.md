## Problem

Eight quants want to know their average salary, but nobody wants to reveal their own. How can they do it?

## Solution

1. Quant 1 picks a random number $r$ and passes $r + s_1$ to quant 2.
2. Quant $k$ adds $s_k$ and passes on. Quant 8 returns $r + s_1 + \dots + s_8$ to quant 1.
3. Quant 1 subtracts $r$:
$$\bar s = \frac{(r + \textstyle\sum_k s_k) - r}{8}.$$

Each person only ever sees a masked partial sum.

**Caveat.** Two quants sitting either side of a third (say 2 and 4) could collude: subtract what 2 passed from what 4 received to recover $s_3$. Each quant adding their own random mask and removing it in a second round fixes that.

## Intuition

**Naive approach.** Have a trusted person collect everyone's salary.

**Why it fails.** It needs trust, which the problem rules out.

**The trick.** **Masking:** add noise only you know, and remove it at the end. This is the idea behind secure aggregation, used for example when data vendors anonymise fund holdings before sharing them.
