## Problem

A sultan holds 50 wise men. A glass stands upright. Each minute he calls one man at random (possibly the same man many times; every man is called infinitely often). The man may flip the glass or leave it. At any time a man may declare "everyone has been called at least once". If true, all go free; if false, all die. They may plan once, beforehand. Find a winning strategy.

## Solution

Choose one **spokesman**. Everyone else is a **signaller**.

- **Signaller:** the first time you see the glass **upright**, turn it upside down. After that (or if it's already upside down), do nothing.
- **Spokesman:** whenever you see it **upside down**, turn it upright and add 1 to your count. When your count reaches $49$, declare.

Each signaller flips exactly once, and only when the spokesman has emptied the "mailbox", so no signal is lost or double-counted. Count $=49$ ⇒ all 49 others have been called, and the spokesman obviously has too.

## Intuition

**Naive approach.** Have everyone flip on their first visit, hoping someone can infer the total.

**Why it fails.** Flips overwrite each other; nobody can tell 3 flips from 1.

**The trick.** Break the symmetry: **one counter, many identical senders**, and a channel that holds one message at a time. Senders send once; the counter clears the channel and counts.
