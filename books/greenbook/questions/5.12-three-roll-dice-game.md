## Problem

You may roll a die up to three times. After the first or second roll you can stop and take the number shown (in dollars), or forfeit it and roll again. If you reach the third roll, you get whatever it shows. What is the game worth, and what is your strategy?

## Solution

1. **Roll 3:** forced: $V_3 = 3.5$.
2. **After roll 2:** keep $x$ iff $x > 3.5$ (4, 5, 6):
$$V_2 = \tfrac36\cdot3.5 + \tfrac16(4+5+6) = 1.75 + 2.5 = 4.25.$$
3. **After roll 1:** keep $x$ iff $x > 4.25$ (5, 6):
$$\begin{aligned}V_1 &= \tfrac46\cdot4.25 + \tfrac16(5+6)\\ &= \tfrac{17}{6} + \tfrac{11}{6} = \tfrac{14}3\approx4.67.\end{aligned}$$

## Intuition

**Naive approach.** "Keep anything above average (3.5)."

**Why it fails.** With two rolls still to come, the value of continuing is 4.25, not 3.5; keeping a 4 then is a mistake.

**The trick.** The threshold at each stage is the value of the game from the next stage on, which you only know if you work **backwards**. More remaining options ⇒ higher threshold.
