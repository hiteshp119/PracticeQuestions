## Problem

Blindfolded, you face 1000 coins on the floor: 20 heads up, 980 tails up. You can't feel which side is up, but you may move and flip coins. Split them into two piles with the same number of heads.

## Solution

1. Take any $20$ coins as pile 1. Say it has $m$ heads (unknown).
2. Then pile 1 has $20 - m$ tails, and pile 2 has $20 - m$ heads.
3. Flip every coin in pile 1: its heads become $20 - m$.

$$\text{heads}_1 = 20 - m = \text{heads}_2. \;\checkmark$$

## Intuition

**Naive approach.** Look for a pile size that guarantees 10 heads each.

**Why it fails.** Without seeing, no split can guarantee where the heads land.

**The trick.** Don't aim for 10 and 10. Aim for a **matching expression**. Choosing the pile size equal to the total number of heads makes pile 1's tails equal pile 2's heads, and one flip turns that into equal heads.
