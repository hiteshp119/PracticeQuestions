## Problem

Two bankers each arrive at a station at a uniformly random time between 5:00 and 6:00 am, independently. Each stays exactly 5 minutes, then leaves. What is the probability that they meet?

## Solution

1. Minutes after 5:00: $X, Y\sim U(0,60)$ independent; $(X,Y)$ is uniform on the $60\times60$ square.
2. They meet iff $|X - Y|\le 5$: a diagonal band.
3. Complement: two right triangles with legs $55$, total area $55^2$:
$$\begin{aligned}P &= 1 - \frac{55^2}{60^2} = \frac{(60-55)(60+55)}{3600}\\ &= \frac{575}{3600} = \frac{23}{144}\approx 0.160.\end{aligned}$$

## Intuition

**Naive approach.** "Each covers 5 of 60 minutes, so about $\frac{10}{60}$."

**Why it fails.** Close, but it ignores the edges: near 5:00 or 6:00 there is less room to overlap.

**The trick.** Two independent uniforms = a uniform point in a square; probabilities become areas. In general, for window $w$ out of $T$: $1 - (1 - w/T)^2$.
