## Problem

You toss a fair coin repeatedly. What is the expected number of tosses until HHH first appears? Until THH first appears?

## Solution

**HHH.** States $S, H, HH$; $\mu$ = expected remaining tosses.
$$\begin{aligned}\mu_S &= 1 + \tfrac12\mu_H + \tfrac12\mu_S,\\ \mu_H &= 1 + \tfrac12\mu_{HH} + \tfrac12\mu_S,\\ \mu_{HH} &= 1 + \tfrac12\cdot0 + \tfrac12\mu_S.\end{aligned}$$
Solve: $\mu_{HH} = 1 + \frac12\mu_S$, $\mu_H = \frac32 + \frac34\mu_S$, $\mu_S = 2 + \mu_H = \frac72 + \frac34\mu_S$, so
$$\mu_S = 14.$$

**THH.** States $S, T, TH$ (an H with no T before is useless, so it stays at $S$):
$$\begin{aligned}\mu_S &= 1 + \tfrac12\mu_T + \tfrac12\mu_S,\\ \mu_T &= 1 + \tfrac12\mu_{TH} + \tfrac12\mu_T,\\ \mu_{TH} &= 1 + \tfrac12\cdot0 + \tfrac12\mu_T.\end{aligned}$$
Solve: $\mu_T = 2 + \mu_{TH} = 3 + \frac12\mu_T \Rightarrow \mu_T = 6$; $\mu_S = 2 + \mu_T = 8$.

**Shortcut (card 5.10).** Expected waiting time = sum of $2^k$ over every $k$ where the pattern's first $k$ tosses equal its last $k$: HHH: $8 + 4 + 2 = 14$; THH: $8$.

## Intuition

**Naive approach.** "Every triple has probability $\frac18$, so both take 8 tosses."

**Why it fails.** Patterns that overlap with themselves (HHH) "waste" progress on failure: HHT throws away the two heads. THH can't overlap itself, so nothing is wasted.

**The trick.** Track how much of the pattern you currently hold; a mismatch sends you to the longest suffix that is still a prefix (like the KMP string-search algorithm).
