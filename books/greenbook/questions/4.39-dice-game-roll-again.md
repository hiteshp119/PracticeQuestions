## Problem

You roll a die and are paid its face value. If the roll is 4, 5 or 6 you roll again (and are paid again); once you roll 1, 2 or 3 the game ends after paying that roll. What is the expected total payoff?

## Solution

1. Let $E$ = expected total. Condition on the first roll $Y$:
   - $Y\in\{1,2,3\}$ ($\frac12$): paid on average $2$, game ends;
   - $Y\in\{4,5,6\}$ ($\frac12$): paid on average $5$, then the game starts afresh.
2.
$$E = \tfrac12\cdot2 + \tfrac12(5 + E)\;\Rightarrow\; \tfrac12E = \tfrac72 \;\Rightarrow\; E = 7.$$

**Wald check.** The number of rolls $N$ is geometric with $p = \frac12$, $E[N] = 2$; each roll averages $3.5$: $E = 3.5\times2 = 7$ (card 5.08).

## Intuition

**Naive approach.** Sum over game lengths: $P(N = n)\times E[\text{sum}\mid N = n]$.

**Why it fails.** Given $N = n$, the first $n-1$ rolls are high and the last is low, so the conditional means need care.

**The trick.** A game that "restarts" after an event satisfies an equation in its own value. Write it, solve it.
