## Problem

Ten bags hold 100 coins each. In nine bags every coin weighs 10 g; in the counterfeit bag every coin weighs either 9 g or 11 g. Using a digital scale **once**, find the counterfeit bag.

## Solution

Take $i$ coins from bag $i$, for $\sum_{i=1}^{10} i = 55$ coins. If all were genuine they'd weigh $550$ g. If bag $k$ is fake:

$$W = 550 \pm k.$$

So $|W - 550|$ names the bag and the sign says heavy or light. Any 10 **distinct** coin counts work.

## Intuition

**Naive approach.** Weigh one coin from each bag in turn: up to 10 weighings.

**Why it fails.** One reading must distinguish 10 bags × 2 directions = 20 cases.

**The trick.** **Encode** the bag number in the weight. Distinct multiplicities make each bag's effect unique. Counterfeit coins II (2.29) pushes the same idea further with base 3.
